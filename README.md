# MCA Student Result Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the examination results of 127 MCA students using Power BI and Excel.

The objective is to understand overall student performance, identify subjects with higher numbers of failures, analyze result distribution, and highlight top-performing students.

## Business Problem

The objective of this analysis is to understand:

- Overall student performance
- Number of students who passed, failed, or were absent
- Subjects with the highest number of failures
- Average student marks
- Top-performing students

## Dataset

- Total Students: 127
- Data Source: MCA examination results
- Key Fields:
  - Registration Number
  - Name
  - Marks
  - Pass/Fail Status
  - Failed Subjects

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel – Data Cleaning and Preparation
- Power BI – Data Analysis and Dashboard Development
- Power BI Visualizations – KPI Cards, Pie Chart and Bar Charts

## Key Metrics

- Total Students: 127
- Passed Students: 94 (74.02%)
- Failed Students: 25 (19.69%)
- Absent Students: 8 (6.30%)
- Average Marks: 442.27

## Key Insights

- 94 out of 127 students passed the examination.
- 25 students failed the examination.
- 8 students were absent.
- Data Structures had the highest number of failures with 13 students.
- Practical (Core/Skill) and Computer Organization and Architecture also had relatively high numbers of failures.
- The dashboard highlights the top 10 students based on marks.

## Recommendations

- Conduct additional academic support and remedial classes for subjects with higher failure counts.
- Strengthen practical training and laboratory sessions.
- Provide additional learning support for students struggling with difficult subjects.
- Monitor student attendance and provide appropriate academic guidance.

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

- Total Students
- Passed Students
- Failed Students
- Absent Students
- Average Marks

### Visualizations

- Student Result Distribution
- Students Failed by Subject
- Top 10 Students by Marks

## Project Outcome

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of MCA student examination performance and helps identify overall result trends, challenging subjects, and top-performing students.
