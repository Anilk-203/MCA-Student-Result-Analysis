# MCA Student Result Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the examination results of 127 MCA students. The goal is to understand overall performance, identify the most difficult subjects, and provide actionable recommendations for academic improvement.

## Business Problem
The college wants to understand:
- How many students are passing vs failing
- Which subjects have the highest failure rates
- Where academic support is most needed

## Dataset
- Total Students: 127
- Columns: Registration Number, Name, Marks, Pass/Fail Status, Failed Subjects

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Data Cleaning + Dashboard)
- Data Analysis & Visualization

## Key Metrics
- Total Students: 127
- Passed: 94 (74%)
- Failed: 25 (19.7%)
- Absent: 8 (6.3%)
- Average Marks (Passed Students): 442

## Key Insights
- 74% of students successfully passed the examination.
- **Data Structures** is the most challenging subject (13 students failed).
- Computer Organization and Architecture, Discrete Structures, and Practical subjects also have high failure rates.
- 8 students were completely absent from the examination.

## Recommendations
- Conduct special remedial classes for **Data Structures**.
- Strengthen practical training and lab sessions.
- Monitor and counsel frequently absent students.
- Provide additional academic support for difficult subjects in future batches.

## Dashboard Features
- KPI Cards (Total Students, Pass, Fail, Absent, Pass %, Average Marks)
- Pie Chart: Student Result Distribution
- Bar Chart: Top Failed Subjects
